      What this evidence can tell us

      These are modelled annual indicators at 4 m above ground, not today’s sound or a forecast. A 10 m grid gives finer spatial detail; it does not establish 10 m accuracy.

      Road and rail reference 2021. The airport metadata does not specify its reference period. New retrieval does not make the underlying modelling current.

      Road and rail report from 40 dB for Lden/day and 35 dB at night. Airport cutoffs vary. Unreported cells may be below a cutoff or missing: they cannot be ranked as the quietest places.

      Background sound, loud-event peaks, event counts and quiet intervals remain unavailable. These layers do not represent all ambient sound or personal exposure.

      The evidence download includes original GeoTIFFs, exact request records, metadata snapshots, checksums and construction code. Independent validation and a study-specific method are still needed for research.
