    What this map can tell you

    These are Defra's Round 4 strategic noise maps: modelled annual averages at 4 m above ground, not today's sound or a measurement. The 10 m grid gives fine spatial detail; it does not establish 10 m accuracy.

    Defra publishes road and rail levels from 40 dB (Lden, day) and 35 dB (night). Below that, Defra confirmed the model calculated a level but did not keep it, so those cells are shown as under 40 dB (under 35 dB at night). No finer value exists.

    Aircraft is mapped only around airports. Blank map means not mapped, not quiet.

    Zoomed out, each pixel shows the 10 m cell at its centre, so thin roads can be skipped. Zoom in to see every cell. Clicking always reads the original cell.
