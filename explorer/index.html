Exploratory historical baseline

This release is not yet a validated research exposure dataset. Its source acquisition linkage and reference periods remain unresolved.

What the colour means

The default Together view shows road/rail upper-bound colours with purple hatching wherever qualified aircraft energy is reported. The hatching indicates presence, not loudness. It does not add the sources or claim a total exposure level: their historical reference periods and metric linkage remain unresolved.

Choose Aircraft to colour the mapped aircraft reported lower bound instead. Beige means no reported aircraft energy, not silence; grey marks withheld or coverage-limited evidence. The road/rail upper and aircraft lower values are different types of bounds, not interchangeable estimates. Roads and railways cannot yet be separated in the published historical bands.

Lden is an annual day–evening–night indicator with evening and night penalties. These configured historical values are not measurements of today's sound, total ambient noise or personal exposure.

The lowest category includes places the source maps cannot distinguish below their reporting thresholds. A bound near 43 dB does not mean the place actually sounds that loud. Grey hatching marks withheld evidence. Uncoloured places may be outside coverage; they are not automatically quiet.

A map for many questions

Explore places, compare surroundings, or investigate a question of your own. Industry, neighbours and natural sounds are not represented. Aircraft evidence is visible by default and separately quantified when reported at your selected place. This release cannot establish background levels, event peaks or frequency, quiet intervals, or separate day/night exposure. The map does not establish public access.

Using the data in research

Location downloads preserve stored values and qualifications without inventing precision. A house-price or stress study still needs a traceable source release, appropriate exposure measure and time period, a documented spatial join, and a strategy for censored or missing values. Association does not establish causation.

Map colours are display summaries. Zoomed-out values average acoustic energy over qualified support and exclude unavailable cells. Use the analytical data and a specified area-weighting method for research; do not sample colours or average dB values blindly.
