## Facilitator introduction

“We are testing the app, not you. Please say what you are looking for, what you
expect to happen and what you think the results mean. If something is confusing,
that is useful feedback. You can stop at any time.”

Read one task at a time. Do not explain the meaning of colours, missing data or
noise indicators before the participant attempts the tasks. Do not show them the
success criteria in the [facilitator protocol](EXPLORER_USABILITY_CHECK.md).
If they become stuck, record the difficulty and the exact help provided. Separate
their words from the facilitator's interpretation. Do not change the UI mid-test.

## Questions after the tasks

- Are these current measurements? What led you to that answer?
- What can this map tell you about individual loud events or quiet intervals?
- What is the first thing you would improve?
